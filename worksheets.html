Free Printable Worksheets

Printable communication activities for autistic children. Free for home and classroom use. No account needed.

These worksheets are designed around one big idea: children communicate more when they have a real reason to. Each sheet gives kids a structured way to ask questions, notice the world, and build curiosity on their own terms.

They pair directly with our blog post on helping minimally verbal autistic children communicate. Open any worksheet below, then click Print or Save as PDF. No sign-up, no cost.

Go Find & Ask!

A parent or teacher fills in the name of a person to find and a question to ask. The child goes on a mission, asks the question, and writes down what they hear. Builds initiation and real-world communication practice.

How to Use This Packet

1

Start with Go Find & Ask. Fill in the "Find" and "Ask them" lines before giving the sheet to your child. Use names of real people in their life — family members, teachers, classmates.

2

Use What I Notice mid-week. Sit down together and talk through what they observed. Model your own observations out loud: "Hmm, I notice..." This builds the habit of paying attention.

3

Graduate to My Questions. Once your child is comfortable asking questions on the Go Find sheet, encourage them to generate their own questions. Check off each one they actually ask.
