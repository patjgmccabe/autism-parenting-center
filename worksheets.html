Free Autism Worksheets

Printable speech and communication worksheets for autistic children. Free for home, speech therapy, and special education classroom use.

These free autism worksheets are designed for parents, speech-language pathologists, and special education teachers who want practical, ready-to-print communication activities. Each worksheet focuses on one skill: asking questions, noticing the world, or building curiosity — all of which support speech development and real-world communication in autistic children.

Whether you are looking for autism speech worksheets to use in a therapy session, special education autism worksheets for the classroom, or simple special needs speech activities to try at home, these sheets are free to print and use with no account required. They pair directly with our blog post on helping minimally verbal autistic children communicate.

How to Use This Packet

1

Start with Go Find & Ask. Fill in the "Find" and "Ask them" lines before giving the sheet to your child. Use names of real people in their life — family members, teachers, classmates.

2

Use What I Notice mid-week. Sit down together and talk through what they observed. Model your own observations out loud: "Hmm, I notice..." This builds the habit of paying attention.

3

Graduate to My Questions. Once your child is comfortable asking questions on the Go Find sheet, encourage them to generate their own questions. Check off each one they actually ask.

These worksheets pair with our blog post on reducing question-overload and building real curiosity in autistic children.
