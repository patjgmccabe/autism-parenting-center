Free Autism Worksheets

Printable speech and communication worksheets for autistic children. Free for home, speech therapy, and special education classroom use.

These free autism worksheets are designed for parents, speech-language pathologists, and special education teachers who want practical, ready-to-print communication activities. Each worksheet focuses on one skill: asking questions, noticing the world, or building curiosity, all of which support speech development and real-world communication in autistic children.

Whether you are looking for autism speech worksheets to use in a therapy session, special education autism worksheets for the classroom, or simple special needs speech activities to try at home, these sheets are free to print and use with no account required.

Each worksheet includes a Customize & Print option to personalize the worksheet.

Communication

Go Find & Ask!

A parent or teacher fills in the name of a person to find and a question to ask. The child goes on a mission, asks the question, and writes down what they hear. Builds initiation and real-world communication practice.

5 missions  ·  Grades K–5  ·  Home & classroom
Open & Print → Customize & Print →

My Questions

Children write down questions they are genuinely curious about, decide who they want to ask, and write down the answer they receive. Encourages self-directed communication and follow-through.

5 questions  ·  Grades 1–6  ·  Home & classroom
