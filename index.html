Free Tools and Resources for Autism Families

Autism Parenting Center is a free resource hub created by autism parents for parents, special education teachers, and speech-language pathologists who support autistic children.

Our free Social Story Creator is one of the most practical tools we offer. Social stories are short, illustrated narratives that help autistic children prepare for everyday situations, from doctor visits to first days at a new school. You build one by uploading photos and writing captions, then print ready-to-cut cards your child can hold and reference on their own. Parents, SLPs, and special education teachers use it to reduce anxiety and build real-world independence in children with autism spectrum disorder.

We also offer a printable IEP Cheat Sheet that covers key terms, parent rights, and questions to bring to any IEP meeting, free autism communication worksheets designed for home, therapy, and classroom use, and a support organization directory covering government resources, family networks, and advocacy groups across the United States. Our blog publishes practical guides on social stories, special education, and communication strategies for families navigating an autism diagnosis.
