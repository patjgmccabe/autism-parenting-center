First-Then Board Builder

Make a simple first-then board for your child. For each box, pick a ready picture, type your own words, or upload a photo. Print the board, or run it on screen with a timer. Free to use.

🔒 Private: everything you type stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

Tap the step your child does first, type your own, or upload a photo. This is usually the hard or non-preferred task.

Tap what happens right after, type your own, or upload a photo. This is usually something your child likes.

Your board

Here's how it will look. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Show it to your child before the task starts.

Tip: in the print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" to keep a digital copy.

Or run it with the timer

Set a time for the First activity. Your child sees the board and the countdown together on this screen. When the time runs out, the First box is marked done and the Then box lights up: time to move on.

  1. Pick how many minutes the First activity gets.
  2. Tap Start Timer and keep the screen where your child can see it.
  3. When the time runs out, First is done and the Then box lights up. Move on to Then.
